# Macrophone — who it's for, the job, the words (Oct 14 2026)

Sameer: "the value prop of being able to quickly and easily control agents and other apps on your
Mac with your iPhone and voice … think of the jobs to be done or personas."

## The people

1. **The agent runner.** A developer with Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or Gemini CLI working on a
   Mac mini or a docked, lid-shut MacBook. Several sessions at once. Steps away all the time —
   coffee, kids, a meeting, the couch.
2. **The couch builder.** Indie hacker or PM who builds with agents in the evening. Ideas arrive
   away from the desk; the laptop is closed.
3. **The Mac-as-home-base person.** Their Mac holds Messages, notes, mail and long-running apps.
   It's across the room more often than it's in front of them.

## The jobs

- **When an agent stops to ask me something, I want to see it and answer in seconds — so it
  doesn't sit idle until I'm back at my desk.** (The big one. Today: an approval prompt waits an
  hour; you walk back to press Return.)
- **When I think of the next thing, I want to tell my agent right then, in my own words — without
  opening a laptop or typing a paragraph on a phone keyboard.**
- **When I'm away from the desk, I want to check what my Mac is doing and nudge it — switch
  sessions, scroll back, stop it — without a remote desktop that shrinks the whole screen.**
- **When I need any app on my Mac — a message, a note, a closed app — I want to reach it from my
  phone.**

What they're firing: walking back to the desk; remote-desktop apps (tiny whole-screen view, a
fake mouse); typing long prompts on a phone; agents waiting.

## The promise

**Steer your Mac from your iPhone — with your voice.** Your agents never wait on you again.

Proof, in the order people care: see any app's window live · tap and swipe it like you're there
(Esc, ↑, ↓, Return for agents' menus) · say the next prompt — cleaned up, pasted and sent · open
any app · works with every agent and every app with somewhere to type · on-device, no account,
nothing collected, open source.

## Words to use

- Hero: **"Steer your Mac from your iPhone."** Sub: **"Your agents never wait on you again."**
- Name the breadth on the web (not in App Store metadata): Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI,
  ChatGPT, Claude, Messages, Notes, Terminal — "or any app on your Mac".
- Verbs: see · tap · swipe · say · send · open. Never "remote desktop", never "screen sharing".
- App Store (no third-party names in name, subtitle, keywords or promotional text): subtitle
  "Control your Mac by voice"; the description says "coding agents in a terminal, an AI app or
  your editor" and "any app with somewhere to type".
