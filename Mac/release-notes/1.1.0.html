Macrophone 1.1.0

Your whole Mac, from your iPhone.

  • Use any window live on your iPhone: a trackpad with a pointer, a full keyboard that types straight into your Mac, and the mic. Menus, pop-ups and system panels show up and take your taps.
  • The whole screen, on any display, and every window on your Mac — dialogs too.
  • Send to lists every open app. The words go into whatever text box you tap.
  • Mac Controls: volume, brightness and play/pause in one button.
  • Hear your Mac on your iPhone (off until you turn it on).
  • Save photos and files from your iPhone to your Mac's Downloads.
  • A Permissions step in setup that shows what's allowed, live.
  • Steadier video away from home, and faster clicks and dictation everywhere.
  • A new icon.
