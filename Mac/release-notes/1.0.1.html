Macrophone 1.0.1

Steer your Mac from your iPhone.

  • Swipe to scroll: on your iPhone, swipe across the window you're watching and it scrolls on your Mac, right where your finger is.
  • Watching any app's window live on your iPhone, tapping to click there, Esc / ↑ / ↓ / Return, and opening any app on your Mac all work with the iPhone app's first App Store version.
