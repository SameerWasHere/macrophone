Macrophone 1.1.22

A big one: your Mac's windows feel at home on your iPhone, and your Mac can listen at the desk.

  • Windows look and feel like they run on your iPhone: their real rounded corners, a sharper picture, and much quicker response while you tap, type and scroll.
  • Dictate at your desk: hold 🌐 and talk, and the cleaned-up words land where you're typing. Or turn on the wake word and just say “Hey Mac”.
  • Keep Awake: your Mac stays on with the lid closed, so you can use it from your iPhone.
  • Alerts that find you: when a chat answers or an app asks a question, your iPhone hears — and you can answer from the notification.
  • A new look that follows light and dark, a simpler setup, and a guided way to use it away from home.
  • Many fixes: dictations survive a dropped connection, a retry right after a blip still pastes and sends, and a second document no longer covers the first.
