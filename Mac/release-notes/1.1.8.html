Macrophone 1.1.8

Your iPhone hears when a window needs you.

  • Put any window on watch, from your iPhone or from the menu bar's “Alert Me When … Changes”. When it finishes, has something new, asks a question or opens a dialog, your iPhone gets a notification with a picture of the window.
  • The notification says what happened in plain words (“Build failed”, “Upload complete”), written on your Mac from the window's own text.
  • Agents asking you something now arrive the same way, with a picture of their window.
  • A window that keeps changing calms down by itself: you hear only when it stops. A watch ends when the window closes, or after twelve quiet hours.
  • While a window is on watch your Mac stays awake (the screen may sleep), so an overnight job still reaches you.
