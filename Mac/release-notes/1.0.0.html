Macrophone 1.0

Your iPhone, as a microphone for your Mac.

  • Speak on your iPhone, pick any app open on your Mac — Claude, Terminal, Messages, anything with a place to type — and the words are pasted there, or pasted and sent.
  • Or send it to the clipboard, or to wherever you're typing.
  • Cleanup on your iPhone with Apple's on-device model: Off, Light, Medium, Heavy, or your own Custom instructions.
  • A 2.5-second countdown you can cancel before anything is sent, never into password fields, and your clipboard comes back afterwards.
  • For developers: a small local API, the mp command-line tool and an MCP server.
