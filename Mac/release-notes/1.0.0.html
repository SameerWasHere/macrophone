Macrophone 1.0

Your iPhone, as a microphone for your Mac.

  • Dictate on your iPhone; the words land on your Mac's clipboard, in the app you're using, or in a script.
  • Profiles: paste into a specific app like Claude, press Return in apps you allow, wrap text in a template, run a script or post to a webhook.
  • A 2.5-second countdown before anything is pasted, never into password fields, and your clipboard comes back afterwards.
  • A local API, the mp command-line tool and an MCP server, so agents can read what you say.
